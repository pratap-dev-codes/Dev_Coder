FAQs

How do I withdraw my earnings?

Go to your Dashboard and click on the Withdraw button. Select your preferred payment method (Easypaisa, Jazzcash, or Upaisa), enter the amount (minimum ₹500), account details, and submit your request. Withdrawals are processed within 24 hours.

How long does task approval take?

Most tasks are reviewed within 24-48 hours. You'll receive a notification when your submission is approved. If additional verification is needed, our team may contact you via WhatsApp or email.

What if my task is rejected?

If your submission doesn't meet requirements, you'll receive a reason for rejection. You may resubmit with proper proof if the task is still available. Repeated rejections may affect your account rating.

How to switch to Publisher mode?

Go to your Profile page and click "Switch to Publisher Mode". You'll need to complete publisher verification first. In publisher mode, you can create tasks and monitor their completion.
